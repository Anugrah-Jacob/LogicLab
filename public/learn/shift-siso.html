SISO shift register

A shift register is a chain of flip-flops that moves a stored bit pattern one position on every clock pulse.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • Each clock edge moves every bit one stage to the right

How it works

In a serial-in, serial-out (SISO) register the data bit enters the first flip-flop and each clock edge passes it to the next, so it appears at the output after as many clocks as there are stages. It works as a digital delay line.

Try it yourself

Serial-in, serial-out: a bit entered at SI reaches SO after four clock edges. Open the circuit, click the switches to change the inputs, and watch the LEDs and the waveform panel respond. You can edit the circuit, convert it to NAND-only or NOR-only gates, and save or export your version.
