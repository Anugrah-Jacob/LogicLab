SR latch (NOR)

An SR latch is the simplest memory circuit: two cross-coupled gates that store one bit.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • Q⁺ = S + R′·Q
  • S=R=1 is forbidden

How it works

In the NOR version a 1 on S sets Q to 1, a 1 on R resets Q to 0, and with both inputs 0 the latch keeps its previous value. Driving S and R high together is forbidden because both outputs go low and the final state becomes unpredictable.

Try it yourself

Two cross-coupled NOR gates remember one bit: S sets Q, R resets it, both low holds the value.
