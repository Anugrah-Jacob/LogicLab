Full adder

A full adder adds three bits (A, B and a carry-in) and outputs a sum bit and a carry-out.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • Sum = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin
  • Cout = A·B + Cin·(A ⊕ B)

How it works

The first XOR gate adds A and B, the second adds the carry-in to that result to form the sum. A carry-out occurs when A and B are both 1, or when the carry-in is 1 and exactly one of A or B is 1. Chaining full adders gives a ripple-carry adder of any width.

Try it yourself
