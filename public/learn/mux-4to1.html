4:1 multiplexer

A 4:1 multiplexer routes one of four input bits to a single output, chosen by two select bits.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • Y = I0·S1′S0′ + I1·S1′S0 + I2·S1S0′ + I3·S1S0

How it works

A decoder made of NOT and AND gates enables exactly one of four AND gates for each value of S1 S0. Only that gate passes its data input, and the OR gate merges the four AND outputs into Y.

Try it yourself

Two select lines (S1 S0) pick one of four data inputs; four AND gates and an OR gate do the work. Open the circuit, click the switches to change the inputs, and watch the LEDs and the waveform panel respond. You can edit the circuit, convert it to NAND-only or NOR-only gates, and save or export your version.
