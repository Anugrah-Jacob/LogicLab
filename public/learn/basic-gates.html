Basic logic gates

Logic gates are the building blocks of digital circuits: each one turns one or two input bits into one output bit.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • AND = A·B
  • OR = A+B
  • NAND = (A·B)′
  • NOR = (A+B)′
  • XOR = A⊕B
  • XNOR = (A⊕B)′

How it works

AND outputs 1 only when both inputs are 1. OR outputs 1 when at least one input is 1. NAND and NOR are AND and OR followed by an inverter. XOR outputs 1 when the inputs differ, and XNOR when they match.
