Master-slave JK flip-flop

A JK flip-flop is a clocked memory element that can hold, set, reset or toggle its output.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • J=0,K=0 hold · J=1,K=0 set · J=0,K=1 reset · J=K=1 toggle

How it works

With J=1 and K=0 it sets, with J=0 and K=1 it resets, with both 0 it holds, and with both 1 it toggles on every clock pulse. A single level-triggered latch would toggle repeatedly while the clock is high (race-around); the master-slave arrangement captures the inputs while the clock is high and updates the output only after it falls.

Try it yourself

J=K=1 toggles Q once per clock pulse. The master-slave structure removes the race-around problem. Open the circuit, click the switches to change the inputs, and watch the LEDs and the waveform panel respond.
