4:2 priority encoder

A priority encoder converts the position of the highest active input line into a binary number.

Open this circuit in LogicLab →

Boolean expressions

  • Y1 = I3 + I2
  • Y0 = I3 + I2′·I1
  • V = I0 + I1 + I2 + I3

How it works

If several inputs are 1 at once, the highest-numbered one wins. Y1 is 1 when I3 or I2 is active, Y0 is 1 for I3 or for I1 when I2 is not active, and the valid output V shows that at least one input is active.
