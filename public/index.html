Frequently asked questions

What is LogicLab?

LogicLab is a free digital electronics suite that runs in your browser. You can build logic circuits, simulate them in real time, read truth tables and waveforms, and explore schematic, transistor, breadboard and PCB views.

Is LogicLab free to use?

Yes. The editor, examples and simulator are free and open source. An account is only needed if you want to sync projects between devices or use LogicLab AI.

Does LogicLab work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is cached and projects are saved on your device, so you can keep working without a connection. Signed-in users sync their projects when they are back online.

Which parts and circuits can I simulate?

Switches, clocks, LEDs, voltmeters, AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR and XNOR gates, D, T and JK flip-flops and shift registers, plus ready-made examples such as adders, multiplexers, decoders, latches, counters and shift registers.

Can I share or export my circuits?

Yes. Use Download in the Share menu to save a project file or export the circuit as a PNG image or PDF.

What is LogicLab AI?

An assistant for signed-in users that explains circuits, answers questions and can build and test circuits on the canvas for you.
