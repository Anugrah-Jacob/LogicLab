# Code of Conduct

LogicLab is a friendly place for people who are learning and building electronics. We expect everyone who takes part
in the project (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with beginners;
- give feedback on the work, not the person;
- welcome different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of learning;
- never harass, insult or discriminate against anyone;
- not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules.
Report problems to the maintainers (see the contact in SECURITY.md or the repository description).
This text follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
